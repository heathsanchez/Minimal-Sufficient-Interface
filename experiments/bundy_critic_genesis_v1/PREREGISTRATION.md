# Pre-registered test — contextual proof-critic genesis (2026-10-11)

## Objective
Test whether the source/target/context-of-hole directed residual retained after MSI LemmaSynthesis Target13 suffices to decide which proof repair is lawful when available hypotheses differ. Then test a narrowly declared constructor-mediated extension of the *critic* (not only a lemma or predicate) using reusable equality proof programs.

## Old capability boundary (C0)
The old critic observes the directed term residual, and its proof operations are reflexivity, a single admitted hypothesis, and its symmetry. Old candidate coverage is complete **only for this declared one-edge grammar**, not for all existing Lean or Bundy proof critics.

## Independent truth/checker
Goals are equations of uninterpreted atoms. Valid derivations may use reflexivity, admitted hypothesis, symmetry and transitivity; the Python independent checker validates every proof DAG edge and exhaustively checks Boolean interpretations of every finite fixture. The separate Lean 4 kernel must check the generated concrete proof terms from exact named hypotheses.

## Frozen development/heldout split
Development: a direct hypothesis, a two-edge valid chain, a three-edge valid chain, an invalid disconnected graph, and a same-directed-goal pair distinguished only by scope/hypotheses.
Protected future: fresh atom names with longer valid chains (lengths four through seven), reversed edges, permuted and unrelated premises, and disconnected negatives. The generator may not see those fixtures before its critic program is frozen; stage separation is enforced in the execution driver. The fixtures are internally authored, not an external natural proof corpus.

## Lower meta-substrate and controls
The lower supplied interpreter knows generic `direct`, `compose`, `symmetry`, and `repeat` primitives; it does not contain target-labelled or variable-labelled repair branches. Enumerate a declared finite set of critic program ASTs and choose a minimum by predeclared cost only after the old boundary is checked. Reject incomplete searches as UNKNOWN. Compare old critic, generated critic, strong context-aware stateless reconstruction, and a certified executable-ledger control. Remove the generated program, a relevant hypothesis, and an unrelated hypothesis. Count all candidate, proof, checker and replay work.

## Promotion thresholds
To claim a **bounded critic-construction instance**, require old-language inadequacy under complete C0, a generated executable not extensionally equivalent within declared domain, all Lean kernels green, future transfer, and causal ablation. To claim a **breakthrough beyond supplied meta-language**, additionally require autonomous generation of the constructor semantics themselves (rather than choosing from this predeclared meta-interpreter), and a protected advantage over a strong stateless generator or an independent new proof class. This experiment cannot satisfy the stronger threshold by design; that is a protected negative control, not a defect to conceal.

## Epistemic constraints
No new universal semantic theorem without source-locked Lean CI. A generator candidate is CANDIDATE until verified; failure to find within budget is UNKNOWN_SEARCH. A successful program in the supplied meta-DSL is not unrestricted invention. No external email, PR, official Arena submission, or mainline merge.

`lean/MinimalRepair.lean` proves the universal property implicit in the MSI meet kernel. For

\[
x \preceq y \iff x \wedge y = x,
\]

the update

\[
\boxed{E' = E \wedge K}
\]

refines both the prior interface `E` and verified constraint `K`, and every common refinement of `E` and `K` refines `E'`.

Therefore `E ∧ K` is the greatest lower bound in the refinement order: equivalently, the unique coarsest / least-change state satisfying both requirements.

\[
\boxed{\text{verified constraint} \to \text{unique minimal justified refinement}.}
\]

This is theorem-level and CI checked.

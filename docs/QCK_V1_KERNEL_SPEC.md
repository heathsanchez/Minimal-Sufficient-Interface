## 1. Purpose

QCK means **Quantitative Consequential Kernel**.

QCK is the minimal mathematical constitution for consequence-governed representation and substitution. It answers, under an explicitly declared consequence contract:

1. which source distinctions are operationally irrelevant;
2. what the canonical sufficient replacement state is;
3. whether a proposed operation can lawfully act on that replacement state;
4. what explicit defect is exposed when it cannot;
5. how much additional representational reserve is required to preserve declared future optionality.

QCK does **not** choose what to search for, what repair is cheapest, what authority should be trusted, or what developmental policy should run. Those are downstream responsibilities.

The dependency direction is normative:

~~~text
MSI sufficiency principle
        ↓
QCK Core
        ↓
QCK FiniteLinear
        ↓
QCK API
        ↓
developmental control / MDA / domain adapters
~~~

QCK is constitutional. Developmental policy may consume its judgments but may not redefine them.

## 2. Consequence contract

Let:

- 𝕜 be a field;
- V be the source state space;
- Y be the observation space;
- A be the admitted action alphabet;
- S : A → (V → V) be the linear generator family;
- C : V → Y be the protected observation map.

For a finite action word w, QCK defines wordMap S w as the composite source action, with letters executed left-to-right.

The contract is not merely C. It is the pair of:

- the immediate protected observation C; and
- every admitted finite continuation generated by S.

Therefore the operational meaning of a state difference depends on what the declared future is allowed to do to it.

---

## 3. Canonical consequential nullspace

The central object is:

\[
N_{\mathcal C}
=
\bigcap_{w\in A^*}
\ker(C\circ S_w).
\]

A difference x lies in \(N_{\mathcal C}\) exactly when every admitted finite continuation remains observationally blind to it:

\[
x\in N_{\mathcal C}
\iff
\forall w,\; C(S_w x)=0.
\]

This is the set of distinctions that the declared consequence contract permits QCK to forget.

The nullspace is invariant under every admitted generator:

\[
S_a(N_{\mathcal C})\subseteq N_{\mathcal C}.
\]

This invariance is what allows the source dynamics to descend to the canonical replacement state.

---

## 4. Canonical sufficient representation

QCK defines the canonical replacement state:

\[
R_{\mathcal C}=V/N_{\mathcal C}.
\]

The canonical map is:

\[
q_{\mathcal C}:V\to R_{\mathcal C}.
\]

Every admitted generator descends to a reduced action on the quotient, and the protected observation descends to a reduced observation.

For every finite word w:

\[
q_{\mathcal C}(S_w x)
=
\bar S_w(q_{\mathcal C}(x)).
\]

The corresponding observation is preserved:

\[
\bar C(q_{\mathcal C}(x))=C(x).
\]

Hence the quotient is a lawful substitute for the source under every continuation in the declared contract.

---

## 5. Sufficiency and universal property

A realized linear representation q is sufficient when every admitted source action and protected observation can be reproduced through q.

The frozen QCK theorem surface proves:

\[
\operatorname{Sufficient}(S,C,q)
\Longrightarrow
\ker q\subseteq N_{\mathcal C}.
\]

Therefore any sufficient realized representation must retain at least the distinctions retained by the canonical quotient.

Equivalently, every sufficient realized representation factors surjectively onto \(R_{\mathcal C}\).

This is the precise minimality claim of QCK V1:

> The canonical quotient is the coarsest sufficient linear representation under the declared fixed action/observation contract.

It is not a claim of universal nonlinear minimality, minimum runtime, minimum description length, or autonomous contract choice.

---

## 6. Certified substitution

Let q : V → R be a current representation and let Aop : V → V be a proposed source operation.

The operation is **kernel-stable** exactly when:

\[
Aop(\ker q)\subseteq\ker q.
\]

When q is surjective, this condition is equivalent to the existence of a unique descended operation on R satisfying the intertwining law:

\[
\bar A\circ q=q\circ Aop.
\]

QCK names this successful judgment:

**CertifiedSubstitution**

A certified substitution means:

> the proposed operation has earned the right to act on the current representation without restoring any source distinction that representation erased.

It does not mean that the operation is useful, cheap, globally optimal, or should be promoted into developmental memory.

---

## 7. New-context defect

If kernel stability fails, QCK produces an explicit separating witness:

\[
\exists v\in V,\qquad
q(v)=0
\quad\land\quad
q(Aop(v))\neq 0.
\]

The current representation identifies the difference v, but the proposed operation turns that erased difference into a visible distinction.

QCK names this judgment:

**NewContextDefect**

This is not an arbitrary failure. It is executable evidence that the current representation is insufficient for the newly admitted operation.

A defect licenses downstream developmental work. It does not prescribe one repair.

## 8. Finite-dimensional observable closure

When V and Y are finite-dimensional and A is finite, QCK defines the future-observable dual space:

\[
L_*
=
\operatorname{span}
\left\{
(C\circ S_w)^*(\psi)
:
w\in A^*,\;\psi\in Y^*
\right\}.
\]

Equivalently, begin with:

\[
L_0=\operatorname{range}(C^*)
\]

and iterate:

\[
L_{n+1}
=
L_n+
\sum_{a\in A}S_a^*(L_n).
\]

The frozen finite-linear layer proves that this ascending chain stabilizes and reaches the full future-observable span.

It also proves:

\[
L_*=\operatorname{Ann}(N_{\mathcal C}).
\]

Thus the source directions retained by the canonical quotient and the future observations required by the consequence contract are exact dual descriptions of the same object.

## 9. Executable finite presentation

QCK defines a basis-free finite presentation:

\[
O:V\to L_*^*,
\qquad
O(x)(\phi)=\phi(x).
\]

The finite-linear theorem surface proves:

\[
\ker O=N_{\mathcal C},
\]

and O is surjective.

Therefore:

\[
V/N_{\mathcal C}\cong L_*^*.
\]

The quotient and the retained future-observable presentation are two realizations of the same canonical consequential state.

In particular:

\[
\dim R_{\mathcal C}
=
\dim L_*.
\]

Every sufficient finite-dimensional linear representation has rank at least this canonical rank.

---

## 10. Quantitative operation defect

For a current finite-dimensional representation O and a proposed operation Aop, define:

\[
F_A(x)=\big(Ox,\;O(Aop\,x)\big).
\]

The operation defect is:

\[
\delta_A
=
\rank F_A-\rank O.
\]

QCK proves:

\[
\delta_A=0
\iff
Aop(\ker O)\subseteq\ker O.
\]

Hence zero quantitative defect is exactly the semantic kernel-stability condition.

When:

\[
\delta_A>0,
\]

QCK recovers the same type of separating witness:

\[
\exists v,\quad O(v)=0,\quad O(Aop\,v)\neq0.
\]

So \(\delta_A\) measures the additional finite-dimensional distinction exposed by the proposed operation.

## 11. Snapshot reserve

Let \(N_0\) be the current active nullspace and let \(N_i\) be nullspaces required by a declared finite set of future interfaces.

QCK defines the snapshot-safe forgettable space:

\[
N_F
=
N_0\cap\bigcap_iN_i.
\]

The minimum additional linear reserve dimension needed to preserve those declared future distinctions is:

\[
r_{\mathrm{snapshot}}
=
\dim N_0-\dim N_F.
\]

QCK V1 proves both:

1. a lower bound: any reserve capable of preserving the declared future distinctions must have at least this rank; and
2. attainment: the canonical snapshot reserve map reaches this bound exactly.

This is a mathematical optionality result. It does not by itself specify a runtime storage format.

---

## 12. Maintained reserve

Snapshot reserve is insufficient when the system must continue to evolve before the reserved distinction is needed.

Let \(A_{\mathrm{wait}}\) be the allowed waiting actions. QCK defines:

\[
N_M
=
\bigcap_{w\in A_{\mathrm{wait}}^*}
S_w^{-1}(N_F).
\]

QCK proves:

- \(N_M\subseteq N_F\);
- \(N_M\) is invariant under every waiting action;
- any waiting-action-invariant subspace contained in \(N_F\) lies inside \(N_M\).

Therefore \(N_M\) is the greatest safely forgettable subspace that remains safe throughout the declared waiting dynamics.

The maintained reserve dimension is:

\[
r_{\mathrm{maintained}}
=
\dim N_0-\dim N_M.
\]

The frozen finite-linear layer proves exact attainment and a self-update law for the maintained reserve.

---

## 13. Active / reserve block dynamics

QCK defines:

- a canonical active state;
- a canonical maintained state;
- a maintained-state-to-active projection;
- induced waiting-action updates on both.

The central block law is:

\[
\pi_{\mathrm{active}}\circ T_{\mathrm{maintained}}
=
T_{\mathrm{active}}\circ\pi_{\mathrm{active}}.
\]

Thus the maintained representation can carry extra future optionality while remaining consistent with current active behavior.

This theorem motivates the QCKN runtime distinction:

**ACTIVE | RESERVE | PROVENANCE**

but QCK itself only owns the mathematics of active/reserve sufficiency. Provenance and runtime retention are downstream protocol concerns.

---

## 14. Fixed-vocabulary rank and generated-context growth

For a fixed observable vocabulary, QCK proves the ordinary modular dimension identity and hence submodularity.

However, capability-driven context generation need not satisfy diminishing returns.

The frozen V1 theorem surface includes a finite U/V rank-profile counterexample in which:

\[
g(\{U\})+g(\{V\})
<
g(\{U,V\})+g(\varnothing).
\]

Therefore:

> fixed-vocabulary observable rank is submodular, but developmental context growth may create complementarity.

QCKN must not assume that capability value decomposes into independent marginal gains.

## 15. Public typed outcome vocabulary

QCK API V1 exports the following stable result meanings.

### CertifiedSubstitution

The proposed operation is stable under the current representation.

### NewContextDefect

The operation exposes a distinction erased by the current representation, with a positive quantitative defect and explicit witness in the finite-linear case.

### ReserveRequired

The declared future requires explicit snapshot and/or maintained reserve rank.

### RecoveryUnavailable

The caller requests a distinction that current active state and available retained material cannot reconstruct.

### ImplementationMismatch

The mathematical contract may be sound, but the concrete implementation fails to realize it.

### CertificateInvalid

Purported promotion evidence fails independent checking.

### OutOfScope

The request lies outside the declared consequence contract.

### Unknown

Available evidence is insufficient for a stronger classification.

QCK also exposes a total finite-linear operation classifier:

\[
\operatorname{assessOperation}(q,Aop)
\in
\{\text{certified},\text{defect}\}.
\]

The broader outcome vocabulary exists so downstream developmental control can preserve epistemic distinctions that are not reducible to operation descent alone.

## 16. Relation to MSI

MSI supplied the underlying consequence-governed sufficiency principle.

In a finite relational realization, for protected continuations B:

\[
x\sim_B y
\iff
\forall c\in B,\;c(x)=c(y).
\]

Adding verified separators refines the current equivalence by meet:

\[
E_{t+1}=E_t\wedge K_t.
\]

The QCK linear nullspace is the corresponding quotient-first realization for a fixed linear action/observation contract.

MSI remains valuable as:

- the finite/discrete adapter semantics;
- the historical derivation of the consequential principle;
- a source of falsifiers and developmental experiments.

After QCK V1 freeze, downstream MSI code must not become a competing constitutional semantics for concepts QCK already fixes.

## 17. Separation from developmental control

QCK does **not** choose an intervention.

For example, NewContextDefect may justify downstream consideration of:

- SPLIT;
- EXPAND;
- RESTRUCTURE;
- CONSTRUCT;
- VERIFY.

Which action is cheapest or appropriate is a policy question.

The normative separation is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{QCK classifies what the evidence means;}
\qquad
\text{MDA chooses what to try next.}
}
\]

Similarly:

- QCK does not promote capabilities;
- QCK does not maintain the causal ledger;
- QCK does not define .mg/MG2;
- QCK does not select a verifier authority;
- QCK does not claim that search failure proves impossibility unless completeness is supplied by the calling contract.

## 18. Constitutional invariants

A conforming downstream system must preserve the following distinctions.

1. **Local silence is not global sufficiency.**  
   Failure of one continuation to separate a pair does not show that no allowed continuation can.

2. **Provenance is not recoverability.**  
   Evidence that deletion was safe is not a reconstruction of what was deleted.

3. **Representation growth is not capability-language growth.**  
   Refining a quotient and enlarging the executable constructor language are different events.

4. **Reachability growth is not syntactic formability growth.**  
   A target may become newly reachable without introducing a syntactically new primitive.

5. **Search failure is not expressivity failure.**  
   A completeness certificate is required before non-realizability can license structural expansion.

6. **Successful search is not promotion.**  
   Promotion belongs to independent authority downstream.

7. **Current sufficiency is contract-relative.**  
   A representation may be sufficient under one consequence contract and defective after the contract expands.

## 19. Claim boundary

QCK V1 establishes a bounded mathematical kernel for declared linear operational systems and finite-dimensional quantitative consequences.

It does **not** establish:

- nonlinear or universal operational minimality;
- automatic selection of the correct consequence contract;
- approximate/noisy equivalence as a general theorem;
- minimum implementation runtime;
- universal minimum description length;
- arbitrary ontology invention;
- universal repair optimality;
- open-ended or unbounded self-development;
- that every future context change can be anticipated;
- that every defect admits an affordable repair.

These are downstream research questions.

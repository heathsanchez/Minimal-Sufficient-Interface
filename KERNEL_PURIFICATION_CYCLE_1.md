# Kernel Purification Cycle 1

This experiment asks whether the previously separate identity and composition completion mechanisms are genuinely distinct primitives.

Result criterion: both must factor through one generic verifier-certified failed-endpoint completion with old-structure retention, no unrelated transport, exact failure-signal ablation, and a unique-lift/free universal property.

If Lean accepts the theorem, identity and composition-specific completion logic falls out of the candidate kernel. The remaining distinction is localized to the extractor that turns richer verifier traces into a failed endpoint.

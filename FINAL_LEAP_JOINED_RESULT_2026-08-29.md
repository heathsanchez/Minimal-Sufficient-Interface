## What is not yet proved

This is **not** a theorem that arbitrary mathematics, natural science, or human concepts will be discovered this way. The four lenses in the rank-2 experiment are hand-designed presentations of a shared finite-abelian-group family. The topological lens is a finite/discrete torus, not smooth differential geometry. The candidate family remains bounded and known to the evaluator. Protected intent is still supplied by verifier semantics rather than inferred from unconstrained human goals.

The remaining leap is therefore external validity rather than another synthetic closure trick: apply the same residual-driven protocol to a source domain not constructed to have a known shared latent algebra, with candidate representations not pre-enumerated as the answer family, and require externally checkable novel capability or mathematical discovery.
